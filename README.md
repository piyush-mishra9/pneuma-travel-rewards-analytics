# Pneuma Travel Rewards & Product Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is a Pneuma-inspired Business and Product Analytics case study
built using synthetic data.

The project analyzes user behavior, travel searches, award flight results,
bookings, loyalty points/miles, and subscription revenue to understand how
an award-travel platform can use data to improve product performance and
user experience.

> ⚠️ Disclaimer: This project uses synthetic data created for portfolio and
> learning purposes. It does not contain Pneuma's internal or confidential data.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Award travel platforms deal with multiple data points such as:

- User activity
- Travel searches
- Award flight availability
- Airlines and loyalty programs
- Points and miles
- Booking activity
- Subscription plans

The objective of this project is to analyze these areas and identify
potential business and product opportunities.

---

## 📊 Key Business Questions

The analysis focuses on questions such as:

- Which routes are searched the most?
- Which destinations have the highest demand?
- Which airlines receive the most bookings?
- Which cabin classes are most popular?
- What devices do users primarily use?
- What percentage of searches convert into bookings?
- How many miles are redeemed?
- How much subscription revenue is generated?
- What potential opportunities can be identified from the data?

## 🔍 Key Insights

Based on the synthetic dataset analysis:

### 1. High Route Demand

Delhi → Dubai, Delhi → London, and Mumbai → Dubai are among
the most searched routes.

### 2. Popular Destinations

Dubai, London, and Singapore are the leading searched destinations
in the dataset.

### 3. Cabin Demand

Economy has the highest search volume, followed by Business,
while First Class has comparatively lower demand.

### 4. Mobile Usage

Mobile devices account for the largest share of searches,
followed by Desktop and Tablet.

### 5. Search-to-Booking Funnel

The dashboard shows a Search-to-Booking conversion of approximately
9.63%.

This indicates a potential opportunity to investigate user drop-off
between search and booking.

### 6. Airline Booking Activity

Lufthansa and Emirates are among the airlines with the highest
number of bookings in the synthetic dataset.

### 7. Miles Redemption

Singapore Airlines and Lufthansa have among the highest total
miles redeemed in the dataset.

### 8. Subscription

The dataset contains both Monthly and Annual subscription plans,
with Monthly subscribers forming the larger group.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, potential opportunities include:

### 1. Improve Search-to-Booking Conversion

Analyze the user journey after search to understand where users
drop off before completing a booking.

### 2. Focus on High-Demand Routes

Improve award discovery and recommendations for frequently
searched routes and destinations.

### 3. Improve Mobile Experience

Since mobile represents the largest search device category,
the mobile search and award comparison experience can be
an important product area.

### 4. Improve Loyalty & Redemption Insights

Provide users with clearer comparisons of miles required,
taxes, and potential redemption value.
